M10 fix: single-threaded FTP downloads, longer idle timeout
The Synology vsftpd drops the FTP control channel mid-transfer when rclone
uses its default multi-threaded copy (several concurrent data connections):
"Failed to copy: multi-thread copy: failed to open source: write: broken
pipe". Downloads now use --multi-thread-streams 1.

Also raise the per-IO idle timeout from 60s to 300s (rclone default): the
NAS has longer hiccups than 60s, and a mid-transfer failure lost a ~400 MB
chapter that had to be re-downloaded. --contimeout 15s still kills dead
connections fast.

Verified against the real NAS: 03.mp3 (410 MB) downloads cleanly with the
new flags; the earlier stalls/failures were multi-connection races.
