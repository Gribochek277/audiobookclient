sync --prune: forget books the library no longer has
Renaming books in the library left their old keys in the local store
forever: the sync keeps a record for a book a listing does not mention
(deliberate — an unreachable or partial NAS must not wipe progress), so
the library showed duplicates next to the real books (found on a real
library: six renamed Dune volumes).

- new `LocalStore::prune_missing(&present)` forgets books whose key is
  absent from the given set, with their chapter rows, and returns what
  it removed; idempotent
- new `sync_io::prune_missing(store, listing)` refuses to touch anything
  for a cached (non-authoritative) listing — a cache cannot prove a book
  is gone — and reports the pruned keys
- `SyncReport::pruned` carries them (included in is_empty and in the TUI
  summary line), and `audiobook sync --prune` runs the prune against the
  real listing the CLI already does, printing '🗑 pruned (no longer in
  the library)' per book or 'nothing to prune' when the store is clean
- the default (`audiobook sync`) still reports the books as 'missing on
  NAS (kept locally)' and keeps them; README documents the flag next to
  the reset section

2 new unit tests (the store prune keeps present books, removes chapter
rows and is idempotent; the sync-level prune ignores a cached listing
and then prunes with a live one); live check on an isolated fixture: a
stale 'Old Name' record survived a plain sync ('kept locally'), was
removed with --prune (chapter row too) while the real book kept its
1234 s of progress, and a second run reported nothing to prune.
