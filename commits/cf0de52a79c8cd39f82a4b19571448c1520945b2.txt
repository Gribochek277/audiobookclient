Spectrum analyser, full-height bars, and a resource-lifecycle audit
Visualiser (closes the WMP/kava-style phase 2):
- The book screen draws bars over the WHOLE free area instead of one line,
  so the empty space is gone: each column grows from the bottom, full rows
  use `█`, the top row carries the fractional part as a `▁`-`▇` block.
- Horizontal scrolling is gone. A sliding time-domain window smeared/ripped
  horizontally (the 48 data columns were stretched over ~78 screen columns,
  so the picture jumped 1-2 cells unevenly every frame). The display is now a
  spectrum analyser: pure-Rust iterative radix-2 Cooley-Tukey FFT (Hann
  window, 4096 samples ~186 ms) mapped onto 48 log-spaced bands (40 Hz -
  11 kHz) with dB-normalised heights and a falling-bar decay.
- The synthetic fallback now generates drifting *tones*: the previous slow
  envelope had no energy above 40 Hz, i.e. it rendered as silence.
- The tap's decoder is refreshed every second pump (~5 fps). A full-width
  animated bar chart at 10 fps wrote ~37k terminal characters/s; the analyser
  at 5 fps writes ~7.8k (and ~245/s when paused), which is what the terminal
  and any multiplexer in between actually have to parse.

Resource lifecycle (every helper, thread and connection):
- New `proc` registry: rclone and ffmpeg are adopted at spawn; `kill_all()`
  runs on every exit path (normal quit, error, and a new panic hook that also
  restores the terminal). Previously a quitting TUI orphaned its `rclone
  copyto`, which kept downloading for minutes at ~70% CPU (observed live).
- Downloads are cancellable end to end: `Transport::download_cancellable` +
  the queue's cancel flag stop the in-flight transfer within one poll and
  remove the `.partial`; leaving a book no longer keeps the NAS busy.
- The ffmpeg tap keeps its child in a shared slot, so dropping the tap kills
  the decoder even if the worker is wedged in a pipe read (it used to be
  detached and left decoding into the void). A leftover debug `eprintln!` in
  that worker (which corrupts the TUI in raw mode) is gone.
- Exit sync is bounded (30 s) and actually returns on timeout: it used to
  print "timed out" and loop forever, hanging the shell after the TUI closed.
- The playing position is autosaved to the local store every 30 s; before,
  it was only written when leaving the book, so a crash/kill lost the whole
  session (the app exists precisely to remember where you stopped).

Tests: 142 lib (+ spectrum/FFT band tests, cancel-kills-the-helper, no-partial
-after-cancel, process registry, autosave, navigation during sync) plus the
integration suites; fmt/clippy/test green; live E2E on the NAS library.
